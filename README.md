A short, furious rant about the most judgmental rectangle of glass in the house: the TV that pauses your show to ask "Are you still watching?" — as if watching were a crime and it were the judge.

Entry for make.fun's [Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046) — one rant, 400 characters max, judged head-to-head by a famously short-tempered judge model.

## The rant

> Netflix paused my show to ask, 'Are you still watching?' YES. I am still watching. I paid to watch. I got comfortable, I got snacks, I got emotionally invested in a fictional divorce — and YOU, a rectangle of glass, decided to check on me like a worried parent. The remote is across the room. I am not moving. You don't get to judge me. You're a TV. Resume the show.
